1. Predict locally

The instant you press a key, Risto applies that input to your copy of the sim. You never wait a round trip to see yourself move.

2. Reconcile the truth

The host is still boss. When its snapshot lands, unacked inputs replay on top of it. A correction smoother hides the one-frame disagreement.

3. Interpolate everyone else

You can't predict the bot's next input, so Risto renders it slightly in the past and blends between snapshots. Motion stays smooth under jitter and loss.

4. Shadow Body

The predicted disc is what you feel. The hollow disc is what the world hits. Other libraries hide that split. Risto puts it on the field — high ping is a skill, not just a slider.
